about=02H3Forge is a right-docked Uber H3 hexagonal spatial synthesis and multi-criteria overlay studio for QGIS. It generates H3 hexagonal tessellations across any study area, scores proximity rings, aggregates heterogeneous vector and raster layers into H3 cells, normalizes criteria onto a 0-100 scale with Benefit and Cost polarity, and combines them into composite vulnerability, hazard, risk, susceptibility, and suitability maps.
    STAGE 0 - H3 GRID AND MULTI-RING BUFFERS: Generate Uber H3 hexagonal grids across a study area polygon, layer extent, or map canvas (Resolutions 7 to 12; defaults to H3 Resolution 10 at approximately 1.50 hectares, down to H3 Resolution 11 at approximately 25 meter edge length). Construct graduated multi-ring proximity buffers (such as 0-100m, 100-250m, 250-500m around power lines, faults, streams, or roads) to assign critical threshold scores (0-100) and optional hard exclusion masks (constraint_mask = 0).
    STAGE 1 - MULTI-SOURCE SPATIAL AGGREGATION: Aggregate Points (count, density per hectare, sum, mean, min, max, median, nearest distance), Lines (total length, density m/ha, length-weighted mean, nearest distance), Polygons (area share, area-weighted mean, area-weighted sum, intersecting count), and Rasters (zonal mean, median, min, max, standard deviation) directly into H3 hexagon attributes.
    STAGE 2 - CRITERION NORMALIZATION (0 TO 100): Transform raw H3 columns onto a unified 0-100 scale with explicit Benefit (+) and Cost (-) polarity using Min-Max Linear, Robust Percentile (P2-P98), Fuzzy Linear, Fuzzy Sigmoid, Fuzzy Gaussian, or Direct 0-100 Multi-Ring Scores.
    STAGE 3 - COUPLED WEIGHTS AND OVERLAY: Adjust interactive weight sliders that automatically rebalance unlocked criteria so the total weight always equals 100, or compute weights in one click with Equal (1/N), CRITIC, Shannon Entropy, PCA, or an interactive AHP Pairwise Matrix (CR <= 0.10). Combine criteria with WLC, OWA, TOPSIS, or VIKOR, with optional H3 1-ring spatial contagion smoothing.
    STAGE 4 - EXPLAINABILITY, UNCERTAINTY AND REPORTING: Inspect each hexagon's primary local driver (top_driver and driver_share), run Monte Carlo weight perturbation (mc_std and rob_score), apply 5-class graduated thematic symbology, and export CSV tables and standalone HTML executive reports.
    Manual: https://geophilo.com/zero2h3forge/manual.html
